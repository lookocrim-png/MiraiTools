How It All Started

The origin of MiraiTools and why we built it.

It all started in late 2023. Our dev team, consisting of Vbxzz, Rizve, and Yuno, were fed up. We were tired of the constant cycle: downloading a tool, being forced through five different ad-walls, only for the application to crash the second we tried to inject a simple script. The community was settling for garbage, and we knew we could do better.

We wanted to build something that felt like a professional developer tool, not a sketchy malware client. We completely scrapped the standard WinForms templates everyone else was using and built our UI from the ground up using PyQt6. We focused heavily on workflow—adding features like a dedicated Lua IDE, a live Client Manager, and background process scanning.

Fast forward to today, and MiraiTools has evolved into a sleek, optimized, and entirely keyless experience. We don't believe in holding your workflow hostage behind paywalls or ad-links. We just want to provide the smoothest, most aesthetically pleasing execution environment on the market.
